# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban 2.0 (Interest Subsidy Scheme)

## Overview

| Sno | Particulars | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Objective | Provide affordable housing to urban under Economically weaker section (EWS), Low Income group (LIG), and Mid Income Group (MIG) categories) |
| 2 | Annual Household Income | EWS :- Up to Rs. 3 lakh; LIG :- Up to Rs. 6 lakh; MIG :- Up to Rs. 9 lakh; For identification as an EWS/LIG/MIG beneficiary under the Scheme, an individual loan applicant will submit self-certificate/affidavit as proof of income. |
| 3 | Loan property limits | Maximum Loan: Rs. 25.00 Lakh; Maximum Housing Value: Rs. 35.00 Lakh; Maximum Carpet Area: 120 Sqm |
| 4 | House Ownership | Applicants must not owning a pucca house anywhere in India on his/her name or any other family members. ( An undertaking from the beneficiary will be taken as a declaration) |
| 5 | Key conditions | Adhaar requirement: - Mandatory for all beneficiaries. Self-Undertaking: - Required as per the format; Registration: - Beneficiaries must register their demand through a Unified web-portal. |
| 6 | Subsidy Details | A maximum release of interest subsidy of Rs. 1.80 lakh having maximum NPV of Rs. 1.50 lakh (at Discount Rate of 8.5%) shall be provided to eligible beneficiaries having a loan tenure of more than five years. Households having income up to Rs. 9 lakh, loan value up to Rs. 25 lakh for property value up to Rs. 35 lakh would be eligible for a subsidy at 4.0% on first Rs. 8 lakh for a tenure up to 12 years. Subsidy to the beneficiaries will be released in 5 equal yearly instalments. |
| 7 | Approved layout plans | The subsidy will be provided to those beneficiaries who have submitted the approved layout plan to the Banks as per guidelines of States/UTs. However, the layout plan may not be insisted by Banks where States/UTs have provided the exemption for construction of houses having provision of deemed approval or pre-approved building plan. |

| Eligibility Criteria | Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) | Low-Income Group (LIG) | Middle-Income Group (MIG) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Household Income (₹) | Up to Rs. 3 lakh | Rs. 3 lakh - Rs. 6 lakh | Rs. 6 lakh - Rs. 9 lakh |
| Interest Subsidy (% p.a.) | 4% on the first Rs. 8 lakh of the loan | 4% on the first Rs. 8 lakh of the loan | 4% on the first Rs. 8 lakh of the loan |
| Maximum Home Loan Eligible (₹) | Rs. 25 lakh | Rs. 25 lakh | Rs. 25 lakh |
| Maximum House Value (₹) | Rs. 35 lakh | Rs. 35 lakh | Rs. 35 lakh |
| Maximum Carpet Area(sqm) | 120 sqm | 120 sqm | 120 sqm |
| Maximum Benefit of Interest Subsidy (₹) | Rs. 1.80 lakh | Rs. 1.80 lakh | Rs. 1.80 lakh |
| Current Home Ownership | NO | NO | NO |
| Property Location | Statutory towns as per Census 2011 or any town subsequently notified by the government |  |  |
