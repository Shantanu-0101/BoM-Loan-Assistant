# Maha Bank Personal Loan scheme to Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) Employees

## Overview

| Particulars | Scheme guidelines |
|---|---|
| Name of the scheme | Maha Bank Personal Loan scheme |
| Purpose of loan | To cater to the requirements of meeting personal expenses. |
| Eligibility | All confirmed employees of BPCL -public sector unit. |
| Min Annual Income | Minimum annual Income - 3.00 Lakhs. |
| Maximum Quantum of Finance | Maximum Rs. 20.00 Lakhs. Loan amount is 20 times of Gross Monthly Income or as per Maximum Quantum of Finance whichever is lower |
| Margin | Nil |
| Repayment Period | 84 months |
| Rate of Interest | RLLR + 1.65% |
| Deduction | Not to Exceed 60% of the Gross Income including Proposed EMI / Notional Interest. (In case of existing Housing loan borrowers 65% of Gross income including proposed EMI) |
| Security | Nil (Clean Loan) |
| Guarantor | One Guarantor acceptable to the Bank |
| Processing Fee | Nil |
