The Bank of Maharashtra gold loan interest rate is applicable based on the selected gold loan scheme, loan amount, tenure and prevailing Bank terms. Customers should check the latest gold loan interest rate per annum before applying, as rates may change.

Gold Loan Interest Calculation
Gold loan interest depends on the loan amount, applicable interest rate and repayment period.
Illustrative formula:
Interest = Loan Amount × Interest Rate × Time
For example, on a Rs. 1,00,000 loan at an illustrative 9% annual interest rate for one year, the simple interest would be Rs. 9,000. Actual interest may vary based on the Bank's applicable calculation method and repayment schedule.

Gold Loan Rate Per Gram
The gold loan rate per gram refers to the loan amount that may be available against eligible gold. It depends on factors such as gold purity, valuation and applicable LTV norms. It is different from the gold loan interest rate.

Gold Loan EMI
