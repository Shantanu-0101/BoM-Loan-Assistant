# Mahabank Education Loan

## Overview

Bank of Maharashtra Education Loan provides financial assistance to eligible students pursuing higher education in India and abroad. Depending on the applicable education loan scheme, the loan can cover expenses such as tuition fees, hostel fees, books, laptops/computers and other eligible education-related expenses. Bank of Maharashtra currently provides education loan options including the Model Education Loan Scheme, Maha Bank Skill Loan Scheme and PM-Vidya Laxmi Education Loan Scheme.

Interest Rate: 7.10% p.a. *

### Education Loan schemes:

- Model Education Loan Scheme
- Maha Bank Skill Loan Scheme
- PM - Vidya Laxmi: Education Loan Scheme

## Features & Benefits

- Low EMI
- Higher Loan Amount
- Track my Loan
- Simplified Disbursement
- No Hidden Charges
- No Prepayment Penalty

- No Margin Money. Up to 100% financing (for Premier institutions)
- No collateral security (for Premier institutions).
- ROI concession to Girl student.
- Instant In-principle sanction before registration in Institutions.
- Longer repayment period upto 15 years (Excluding Moratorium) to reduce the EMI burden.
- Loan will cover tuition fees, hostel fees, cost of books etc.

## Check Eligibility

Eligibility for a Bank of Maharashtra Education Loan depends on the education loan scheme, course, institution, study location and applicable bank guidelines.

### Basic Eligibility

Applicants should generally meet the applicable requirements relating to:

- Nationality: Eligibility is subject to the requirements of the applicable education loan scheme.
- Admission: The student should have secured admission to an eligible course or institution.
- Course: The course should fall within the courses covered under the applicable education loan scheme.
- Institution: The educational institution should meet the recognition or eligibility requirements applicable to the selected scheme.
- Academic requirements: Academic records and qualifying examination results may be required.
- Co-applicant: A parent or natural guardian generally acts as co-applicant/co-borrower. For a married student, the applicable co-obligor may include the spouse or parent(s)/parent(s)-in-law, as specified by the bank.

### Scheme-Specific Eligibility

Eligibility varies by scheme. Applicants should check the detailed terms of the applicable scheme before submitting an application.

## FAQs

### What is Education Loan and how does it work?

Education Loan is meant to provide financial assistance/ support to meritorious student for pursuing higher studies /education in courses conducted by the Premier Educational Institutions in India such as IIMs, ISB, IITs, NITs, XLRI, MBBS, Medical College etc.

Apart from these, Education Loans are also provided for Graduation courses/Colleges under Universities approved by UGC. Other courses leading to diploma / degree etc. conducted by colleges / universities approved by UGC / Govt. / AICTE / AIBMS / ICMR etc. in India.

For Studies Abroad, Education Loan is provided for Job oriented Professional/Technical / PG courses/ Post Graduation - MCA, MBA, MS etc.

### What are the variants of Education Loans provided by Bank of Maharashtra?

Bank of Maharashtra provides three variants of Education Loans as under:

- Maha Scholar Education Loan -Loan for pursuing higher studies in Premier Institutions in India
- Model Education Loan- Loan for pursuing higher studies in India and abroad for recognized universities
- Mahabank Skill Loan- Loan for Skill development courses
- Maha Scholar Overseas Education loan- Loans is granted to deserving/meritorious students for pursuing full-time regular courses in premier foreign colleges/universities

### Who can apply for Bank of Maharashtra Education Loan?

Eligible applicants include students seeking education at recognized institutions, both in India and abroad. The loan is open to Indian nationals with a secured admission offer.

### What are the documents required for Education Loan?

You will need to furnish the following documents along with the completed application form. When the loan is jointly taken, relevant information would relate to the guardian and the student both.

- Mark sheet of last qualifying examination for school and graduate studies in India
- Proof of admission to the course
- Schedule of expenses for the course
- 2 passport size photographs
- Statement of Bank account for the last six months of borrower
- Income tax assessment order not more than 2 years old
- Brief statement of assets and liabilities of borrower.

For more details, please check "Document Required" section

If you are not an existing bank customer you would also need to establish your identity and give proof of residence.

### Who can be a co-applicant in Education Loan?

Co-applicant should be parent/natural guardian of the student borrower. In case of married person, co-obligator can be spouse or the parent(s)/parents in law.

### Is there any requirement for collateral or a co-borrower for Bank of Maharashtra Education loans?

- As per Model education loan scheme devised by IBA and subsequently implemented by our bank, no collateral security is required for loansupto Rs. 7.50 lakhs and for loan above Rs. 7.50 lakhs, acceptable tangible security with sufficient margin is insisted from the borrowers.
- Also, under "Maha scholar education loan scheme" of our Bank for students taking admission in Premier Institutes of India, no collateral security is required for loan upto Rs. 40.00 lakhs.

### Are there any processing fees on Bank of Maharashtra Education Loans?

There is no processing fee irrespective of loan amount for Mahabank Scholar Loans for premier Institutes. For Mahabank Model Education Loans, there is a minimal Processing fee of 0.50% of the loan amount in case of studies abroad only& is refundable in case borrower takes up the course.

### What is the repayment schedule for Bank of Maharashtra Education Loans?

Education loan is being extended for 15 years of tenure. The repayment would begin after one year of the course period or six months after you get a job, whichever is earlier.

### What expenses does the Bank of Maharashtra education loan cover?

Bank of Maharashtra education loan covers various expenses like tuition fees, hostel fees, cost of books, Laptop/Computer cost etc.

### What is the margin money required for Bank of Maharashtra Education Loans?

For Maha Scholar Education Loan, there is no margin required for Premier Institutions listed under List 'A'. For List 'B' & 'C' category Institutes, a minimal 5% margin of the loan amount is required. Under Model Education Loan uptoRs. 4 lakhs, there is no margin required. Above 4 lakhs, there is a required of 5% margin for studies in India and 15% for Studies abroad.

### Does Bank of Maharashtra offers any special interest rates concessions for education loans?

Yes, under Maha Scholar Education Loans, there is a concession of 0.10% in interest rate to Girl students. However, under Model Education Loan scheme, Bank offers interest concessions keeping the maximum concessions upto 0.50% for the following-

- 0.50% concession for girl students
- 0.50% concession to our existing Housing Loan borrower
- Up to 0.25% for meritorious students.

## How to Apply

Step 1: Check Eligibility
Check the eligibility requirements of the applicable Bank of Maharashtra education loan scheme and confirm that your course and institution meet the applicable criteria.

Step 2: Select the Applicable Scheme
Choose the education loan scheme applicable to your course, institution and study location.

Step 3: Prepare Documents
Keep your admission, academic, KYC, income and other applicable documents ready.

Step 4: Submit the Application
Submit the education loan application through the applicable application channel.

For PM-Vidya Laxmi, the Bank's current scheme page provides a unified three-step portal process:

1. Register on the PM-Vidya Laxmi portal.
2. Fill the Common Education Loan Application Form and search for an applicable education loan.
3. Submit the application to the selected bank.

Step 5: Application Evaluation
The Bank evaluates the application based on the applicable scheme requirements, documents, eligibility and credit requirements.

Step 6: Sanction and Disbursement
After sanction, the education loan is disbursed according to the applicable terms and requirements.
