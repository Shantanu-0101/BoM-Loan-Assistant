# Collateral free Term Loan facility

## Overview

- Term loan facility for business purpose; Maximum Rs. 5.00 crore
- Security: Assets of the borrower as per definition of CGTMSE
- No collateral security [Mandatory CGTMSE cover]
- Minimum 25% margin
- Repayment period upto 7 years including moratorium period
