#### Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for an education loan for higher studies?
Students who secure admission through merit or an entrance test in recognised institutions in India or abroad are eligible. A parent or guardian joins as co-applicant. This makes the scheme suitable for both domestic education financing and a study abroad loan requirement.

Which courses are covered under the scheme?
The loan supports graduation, post-graduation, professional, and technical programmes approved by UGC, AICTE, and other authorised bodies. It is designed for students planning career-focused higher education in India or overseas.

What is the maximum loan amount I can get?
You can avail up to Rs. 10 lakh for studies in India and up to Rs. 20 lakh for education abroad, based on eligibility and course requirements.

Do I need collateral for the loan?
No security is required for loans up to Rs. 7.5 lakh. For higher amounts, collateral is needed as per bank norms.

What expenses are covered in a study abroad loan or higher education loan?
The loan covers tuition fees, accommodation, books, travel expenses, computers, and other academic essentials so that students focus on learning without financial stress.

When does repayment start?
Repayment begins after the course period plus the moratorium. The flexible tenure helps students manage repayment comfortably after employment.

Are there any benefits for students?
Interest concessions are available for eligible applicants, especially for girl students and those who service interest during the study period.
