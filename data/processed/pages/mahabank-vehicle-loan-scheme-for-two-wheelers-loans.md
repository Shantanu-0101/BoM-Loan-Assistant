## Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the Mahabank Two-Wheeler Loan Scheme?
It is a loan offered by Bank of Maharashtra to help individuals finance the purchase of new two-wheelers, including bikes and electric vehicles.

2. Who can apply for this loan?
Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, business owners, farmers, and pensioners (with eligible income) can apply.

### 3. What is the minimum income required?

- Salaried: Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum
- Self-employed/others: Rs. 3 lakh per annum

### 4. What is the loan amount available?

- Minimum: Rs. 50,000
- Maximum: Up to Rs. 5 lakh (regular bikes)
- Up to Rs. 25 lakh (high-end bikes)

5. What is the repayment tenure?
The loan can be repaid over a maximum period of 60 months (5 years).

6. How much margin (down payment) is required?
A minimum of 15% of the on-road price is required as margin.

Interest rates start from around 11.20% p.a. onwards, depending on credit profile and CIBIL score.

8. Are electric two-wheelers eligible for financing?
Yes, loans are available for electric two-wheelers under green vehicle financing.

9. What security is required for the loan?
The purchased vehicle is hypothecated to the bank as collateral until the loan is fully repaid.

10. How can I apply for the loan?
You can apply online through the Bank of Maharashtra website or visit your nearest branch for assistance.
