# Maha Super Car Loan

## Overview

The Maha Super Car Loan from Bank of Maharashtra is a loan scheme for financing the purchase of a new car, Jeep, Multi Utility Vehicle (MUV) or similar new four-wheeler. The scheme offers financing of up to 90% of the vehicle cost, repayment tenure of up to 84 months and no pre-payment, pre-closure or part-payment charges, subject to applicable terms and eligibility.
