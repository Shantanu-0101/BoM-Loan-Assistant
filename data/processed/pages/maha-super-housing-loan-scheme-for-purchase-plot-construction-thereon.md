# Maha Super Housing Loan Scheme: Purchase of Plot & construction thereon

## Overview

Owning a home starts with the right foundation-both literally and financially. The Maha Super Housing Loan Scheme by the Bank of Maharashtra provides seamless financial support for anyone looking for a home loan for a plot purchase.

Whether you're looking to invest in land or begin construction right away, this home loan scheme ensures that your home ownership journey is stress-free and affordable.

#### Purpose of the Home Loan

This scheme is perfect for those who want a home loan for a plot purchase along with financial assistance for building their home. Instead of applying for separate loans, this scheme simplifies the process by offering a single financing solution for both land acquisition and house construction. With competitive interest rates and flexible repayment terms, the scheme is ideal for first-time home-buyers as well as those upgrading to a larger home.

#### Who Can Apply for this Home Loan Scheme?

The Maha Super Housing Loan Scheme is open to a wide range of applicants, ensuring accessibility for:

- Resident Indians with a stable income.
- Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs), and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) who wish to invest in property back home.

Whether you're purchasing a plot for immediate development or planning future construction, the scheme accommodates different financial needs.

#### Loan Repayment Flexibility

Understanding the financial planning needed for home construction, the scheme provides flexible repayment options:

- For Plot Purchase: EMI payments begin immediately.
- For Home Construction: A moratorium period of up to 18 months is available, allowing you to focus on building before beginning repayments.
- Maximum Tenure: Up to 30 years, ensuring manageable EMIs.

Since home construction takes time, the option of deferred EMIs during the building phase makes this an attractive financing choice for those seeking a home loan for house construction.

#### Affordable Interest Rates & Low Processing Fees

The Maha Super Housing Loan Scheme comes with attractive interest rates, making it one of the most affordable financing options. The processing fee is just 0.25% of the loan amount, capped at Rs. 25,000. Additionally, government employees benefit from a full waiver of processing fees when applying for a loan.
